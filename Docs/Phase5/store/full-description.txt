Hold the lighter to the fuse, wait for the hiss, and watch the courtyard light up.

Utsav Nights is a calm festival-night fireworks game for your phone. There are no levels, timers or scores: you choose a cracker from the tray, place it, light it, and enjoy the show.

EIGHT CRACKERS, EACH WITH ITS OWN SOUND AND LIGHT
• Flowerpot (anar)
• Ground spinner (chakri)
• Rocket
• Sparkler (phuljhadi)
• Single bang
• Chain crackers (ladi)
• Skyshot
• Multi-shot cake
Plus the Peacock fountain as a premium cracker.

FINALE MODE
Place up to six crackers and set off a finale. They go off one after another, and you can run it again.

MADE FOR ONE HAND
Portrait layout, a small tray, and big touch targets. Haptics you can feel as the fuse catches and the burst lands, and you can switch them off.

COMFORTABLE FOR EVERYONE
• Quiet mode for late nights
• Reduce screen shake and bright flashes
• Works with TalkBack
• Plays fully offline

FAIR ADS
No ad ever interrupts a fuse, a burst or a finale. Ads only play if you choose to watch one, for example to try the Peacock fountain for 24 hours. A single purchase removes ads and unlocks everything.

Utsav Nights is a game. Real fireworks are dangerous. Always follow local rules and safety advice when celebrating.
