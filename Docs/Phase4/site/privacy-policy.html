Utsav Nights privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE] · Developer: [DEVELOPER NAME] · Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]

Utsav Nights is a festival fireworks simulator for Android. You never need an account to play. An optional Google sign-in exists only for cloud backup and for sharing your home or shows (see below). The game never asks for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. This page explains what data the app and the services it uses handle, and the choices you have.

Data that stays on your phone

The app saves your settings (volume, Quiet mode, vibration, graphics quality, privacy choices) and your progress (crackers you unlocked, and whether you bought "Remove ads"). It also keeps the fireworks shows you design and the clips you record (the newest five). These files stay on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes them.

Backups

Backup file: you can save everything (settings, avatar, house and decorations, layouts, shows) to a file you keep, locked with a password you choose. The file goes only where you put it. Nobody, including us, can open it without the password.

Cloud backup (optional): if you sign in with Google, you can keep up to three backups in your account, made only when you tap Back up now. Each backup is encrypted on your phone before it is uploaded, with a key that belongs to your account, so the storage only holds scrambled data. Purchases are never in a backup; they come back through Restore purchases.

Signing in with Google (optional)

Signing in uses Google Firebase Authentication. Firebase keeps your Google account's ID, email address and name to manage the sign-in. The game itself never reads or shows them, and they are never part of your backups, shares or usage statistics. Settings → Backup and restore → Delete my cloud data deletes your cloud backups, your shared homes and shows, and your Firebase account, and signs you out. What is on your phone stays.

Sharing your home and shows by code

You can share how your home looks (the house, its decorations and neighbourhood) or a fireworks show you designed. Sharing gives you a code. Anyone who has the code can see what you shared, view only. A shared home carries no names and no text, and your avatar's look only if you turn on "Show my avatar". It never carries the avatar's name. A shared show carries its crackers and timing, not its name. You can take a code down at any time from Visit, then Your codes. Codes nobody has visited for a year are deleted.

To visit a home or open a show, the game signs in to Firebase anonymously (no Google account, no personal details). Homes you visit are kept on your phone, the ten newest, so you can see them again offline.

Reports: if you report a code, the report (its reason from a fixed list, and the time) is stored with it. After three reports from different players a code is hidden until we review it, then either kept or deleted.

Suggesting your avatar's look from a photo (optional, 18 and over)

In the avatar creator you can ask for a suggestion from a photo. It is only offered after you confirm you are 18 or older, and never to anyone who says they are younger. You take the photo with your camera app or choose one with your phone's photo picker; the game needs no camera or photo permission. The photo never leaves your phone. The game reads a small copy of it into memory, finds the face with Android's built-in face detector, works out a skin tone, a hair colour and a face shape, and deletes the photo and its copy straight away. Only those three choices are kept, in your avatar like any choice you make yourself, and you can change them. Nothing of the photo is stored, uploaded, shared or included in backups or usage statistics.

Clips you share

A clip is a short video of the game's screen and sound, recorded only when you tap Record or Clip. It never uses your camera or microphone. A clip leaves your phone only when you choose to share it, and then it goes to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp or Instagram), under that app's own privacy policy.

Downloads

Extra neighbourhoods, outfits and shows can be downloaded when you choose to (on Wi-Fi only, unless you change that). The download comes from a file server that, like any website, receives your IP address to send the files. The request contains no account, advertising ID or other identifier.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, but only when you choose to watch one, for example to unlock a premium cracker for 24 hours. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, basic device information, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. It uses this to show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ad loads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play)

"Remove ads and unlock all" is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and the app never sees your card or payment details. The app only learns that the purchase exists, so it can switch ads off and restore your purchase on a new phone.

Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

With your permission, the app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase. These help us fix problems and make the game better. They include events such as "a cracker was lit" or "the settings were changed", which outfit pieces you chose for your avatar, which decorations and house parts you chose and how many decorations your courtyard has, which neighbourhood and celebration spot you chose and how long and how far you walked, which content packs you downloaded or deleted (and whether a download failed), how many crackers and launch pads your saved shows have, and whether you recorded or shared a clip and to which app, whether you signed in, made or restored a backup, shared, visited or reported a home, or shared or opened a show (and whether it worked), which festival you chose, whether you went inside your house and how many decorations a room has, whether a photo suggestion worked (never anything of the photo), the game's performance, the device model and Android version, and an app-instance identifier created by Firebase. They never include your name, contacts or anything you type, never your avatar's name, body shape or skin tone, and never the names of your saved house layouts or shows, where you placed things or where you walked, never the clips themselves, and never a share code, an account ID or anything in a backup. You can switch this off at any time in Settings → Share usage statistics. In the regions listed above, nothing is sent until you have made your consent choice.

Children

Utsav Nights is made for a general audience and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Security and retention

Data sent to Google's services travels over encrypted connections. Google keeps ad and analytics data according to its own retention policies. Usage statistics are kept for 2 months, the shortest setting Google Analytics offers. Cloud backups stay until you delete them (three are kept, older ones are deleted when you back up again). Shared codes stay until you take them down, delete your cloud data, or nobody visits them for a year. Reports stay until the code is reviewed. We have no servers of our own: all of this is in Google Firebase.

Deleting your data

To delete everything stored on your phone, uninstall the app or clear its data. To delete what is in the cloud (backups, shared homes and shows, your Firebase account), use Settings → Backup and restore → Delete my cloud data while signed in.
