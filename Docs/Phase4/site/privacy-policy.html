Utsav Nights is a festival fireworks simulator for Android. It does not ask you to create an account, and it never asks for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. This page explains what data the app and the services it uses handle, and the choices you have.

Data that stays on your phone

The app saves your settings (volume, Quiet mode, vibration, graphics quality, privacy choices) and your progress (crackers you unlocked, and whether you bought "Remove ads"). These files stay on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes them.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, but only when you choose to watch one, for example to unlock a premium cracker for 24 hours. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, basic device information, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. It uses this to show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ad loads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play)

"Remove ads and unlock all" is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and the app never sees your card or payment details. The app only learns that the purchase exists, so it can switch ads off and restore your purchase on a new phone.

Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

With your permission, the app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase. These help us fix problems and make the game better. They include events such as "a cracker was lit" or "the settings were changed", the game's performance, the device model and Android version, and an app-instance identifier created by Firebase. They never include your name, contacts or anything you type. You can switch this off at any time in Settings → Share usage statistics. In the regions listed above, nothing is sent until you have made your consent choice.

Children

Utsav Nights is made for a general audience and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Security and retention

Data sent to Google's services travels over encrypted connections. Google keeps ad and analytics data according to its own retention policies. Usage statistics are kept for 2 months, the shortest setting Google Analytics offers. We have no servers of our own and keep no copies.
