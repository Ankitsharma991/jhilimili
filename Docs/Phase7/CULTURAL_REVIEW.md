# Phase 7 cultural review list

Before release, a reviewer familiar with North Indian, South Indian and Nepali (Tihar) home festivals checks every house
part and decoration below: the name, the shape, the colours, and where the game lets it be placed. The game's shapes are
generic and procedural (LICENCE_LOG own-008). Nothing here copies a real building or a sacred design.

Mark each row **OK**, **Change** (with a note) or **Remove**. Rows marked Change or Remove go into the feedback round
after all PRD features are built.

## Festival filter

- Tihar set: diya, samai, paper lantern, lights, toran, garlands, marigold pot, diya-ring rangoli, mandala floor art. Is
  anything missing that Nepali players would expect first (for example a Laxmi footprint path, or marigold garlands on the
  gate)?
- Is "Everyday" the right word for the non-festival set?

## Placement rules that touch custom

- The doorway is kept clear; rangoli may lie in front of it.
- Lamps cannot go on the house walls, but lanterns can hang from them.
- Nothing may be placed where the player's avatar stands.
